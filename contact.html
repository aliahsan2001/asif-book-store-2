Questions parents ask

How long does delivery take?

Orders confirmed before 2 pm are delivered in Gulshan-e-Iqbal the same day. Other Karachi areas take 1 to 2 working days. Delivery costs Rs 250 and is free on orders over Rs 5,000.

Can I collect my order from the store?

Yes. Choose store pickup at checkout and we'll message you when it's packed, usually within 4 to 6 hours. Pickup is free.

My school isn't listed. Can you still help?

Send a clear photo of the school's book list on WhatsApp. We'll reply with the price and availability of every item, usually within an hour during store hours.

What if a book is the wrong edition?

If we supply an edition that doesn't match your school's list, we replace it free. Bring it to the store or we'll collect it with your next delivery.

How do uniform exchanges work?

Any uniform item can be exchanged for another size within 7 days, as long as tags are on and it hasn't been washed. Hem and sleeve alterations are free within the same period.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery or at pickup, JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfer. We confirm every order by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch.
