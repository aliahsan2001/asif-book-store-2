Delivery, returns and policies

The plain-language version of how we deliver, exchange and look after your information.

Delivery and pickup

We deliver across Karachi. Orders confirmed before 2 pm are delivered in Gulshan-e-Iqbal the same day; other areas take 1 to 2 working days. Delivery costs Rs 250 and is free on orders over Rs 5,000.

Store pickup is free. We'll message you when your order is packed, usually within 4 to 6 hours. Bring your order number.

Returns and exchanges

  • Uniforms: exchange any size within 7 days, unwashed with tags on. Hem and sleeve alterations are free in the same period.
  • Books: if a title doesn't match your school's prescribed edition, we replace it free. Covered or written-in books can't be returned for other reasons.
  • Stationery and bags: faulty items are replaced free within 7 days.

Privacy

We use your name, phone number and address only to confirm and deliver your order. We never sell your details. Saved details on this website stay on your device until our account system is connected.

Terms of sale

Prices are in Pakistani rupees and include all taxes. Orders are confirmed by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch; we'll tell you then if anything is out of stock. Promo codes can't be combined and expire at the end of the session.

Have these policies reviewed by the shop owner before launch.
